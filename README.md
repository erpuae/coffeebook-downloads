# CoffeeBook POS for Android

The CoffeeBook app for cafes and restaurants.

**[Download CoffeeBook.apk](https://github.com/erpuae/coffeebook-downloads/raw/main/CoffeeBook.apk)** (Android 8 or later, about 18 MB)

## Install

1. Open the link on your Android phone or tablet. If your phone warns you about the file, tap **Download anyway**.
2. Open the file. If asked, allow installs from your browser.
3. Open **CoffeeBook**. On the sign-in screen tap **Set server address** and enter your site name.
4. Sign in with your email and password.
